Vortex Hop

Survive as long as you can. The vortex keeps getting stronger, and each fall adds 1×; at 5× the run ends. Grab glowing orbs to calm it: the deeper they are, the more they're worth.

  • One thumb: drag anywhere to walk. To jump, double-tap and hold, then drag the way you want to go: further drag, further jump. Let go to jump, or let go in the red centre to cancel.
  • Tiles crack when you land and break 3.5s later, along the cracks shown. After a break, your piece waits until you move.
  • Small tiles are faster (orange). Tiny gem tiles never break.
  • Controller: left stick walks, right stick aims, A jumps.

Controller connected: press A to play
