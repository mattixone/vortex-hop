Vortex Hop

Hop from tile to tile out of the whirlpool and reach the golden rim.

  • Tap or click to jump. Tiles keep moving while you're in the air, so aim where they'll be.
  • Tiles crack when you land. The cracks show exactly where they'll break.
  • Small tiles are faster and sink sooner. Blue is slow, orange is fast.
  • Green rings are checkpoints. R restarts.
