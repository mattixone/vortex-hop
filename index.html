Vortex Hop

Hop from tile to tile out of the whirlpool and reach the golden rim.

  • Joystick (left thumb) walks. Up is always outward. Walk off an edge onto any tile that overlaps yours.
  • Jump slider (right thumb): slide up to jump further, down into the red to cancel, let go to jump. Tiles keep moving while you're in the air, so aim where they'll be.
  • Tiles crack when you land and break 3.5 seconds later. The cracks show exactly where they'll break.
  • After a break, the piece you're standing on waits until you move, then starts cracking from your feet.
  • Tiny tiles with a white gem inside never break, but they're the fastest thing in the vortex.
  • Small tiles are faster and sink sooner. Blue is slow, orange is fast.
  • Green rings are checkpoints. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, hold Space to jump (Esc cancels), R restarts.
