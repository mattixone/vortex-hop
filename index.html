Singleplayer

Survive as long as you can. The vortex keeps getting stronger, and each fall adds 1×; at 5× the run ends. Glowing orbs calm it: the deeper they are, the more time they buy.

Push battle

Orbs give push charges (up to 3). A push blasts a narrow cone of air in front of you and shoves anyone in it, harder up close. Everyone has 3 falls; the last one standing wins. You can't be pushed in the air or just after respawning.

Tiles

  • Tiles crack when you land and break 3.5s later, along the cracks shown. After a break, your piece waits until you move.
  • Small tiles are faster (orange). Tiny gem tiles never break.

Controls

  • Touch: drag anywhere to walk. To jump, double-tap and hold, then drag the way you want to go: short, medium or long. Let go in the red centre to cancel. In a battle, tap PUSH.
  • Controller: left stick walks, right stick aims, A jumps, X or LB pushes, Start pauses.
  • Keyboard: arrows or WASD walk, hold Space to jump, F pushes, P pauses, R restarts.
